Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty setting, no mission replay. Guides put the platinum at roughly 200 hours, most of it on the 100% checklist. Work the phases in order; almost everything can wait until after the credits — except the one trophy flagged below.

⚠ The one missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation — it is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do not skip it. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission, because there is no mission replay. Every other trophy can be earned after the story.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5

    Finish Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1, 2, 3 and 4 and the final mission — 13 hidden story trophies pop as you go (some land just after the next chapter starts). Take the named missions as they come: Breakfast Run (Ch. 2), The Cartel Heist (Ch. 3), Child's Play, Headliners and VCIA Takedown (Ch. 4), Operation Savage Glory (Ch. 5). Do the character quest lines when they appear — Andy (Lucia-only, Ch. 3), Kenton (shipwrecks from Ch. 1), Billy (after Coffee Break, Ch. 2), Randy (16 sports cars, Ch. 3), Tyrone (Jason-only, Ch. 3) and Bret's hunting — and turn on every progress and collectible notification in Settings. Keep cheats off: using one disables trophies and saving.

  2. Phase 2 — The 100% checklist (gold: A Long Road)

    After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through it — it is the longest stretch, around 100 hours. Required in full: all robberies (8 bank vaults, 39 gang operations), underwater discoveries, all 42 Shooting Range gold medals, all drone races and Bret's 15 Target Decks. Required in part: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 100 WAiNK scans. Trophies that fall out of this phase: Cunning Stunts (250), The Shootist (42 golds), Top Fun (14 drone-race locations), One Last Thing (50 challenges), Maximum Withdrawal (5 vaults), Pest Control (one operation per gang) and Dime on the Dollar ($250,000 laundered).

  3. Phase 3 — Collectibles & counts

    Finish the counting trophies: 3 Collectible Card sets (Don't Touch My Deck), WAiNK scans at 100 locations (You WAiNKer!), 100 vehicles discovered (Whip It Good), all of Leonida explored (The Goodtime State), 8 garages bought (Little Boxes), 10 vehicles registered to Scooter Bros (On the Spot), and the 18 meditation spots (Positive Mental Attitude — see the missable warning above).

  4. Phase 4 — Cleanup & activities

    Eight stranger strands (Strangers & Freaks), all 9 activity types (Master of None), 20 different races (Earn Cash, Not Kudos), the partner trophies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy), combat and robbery one-offs (Gunslinger, Sneakthief, A Classic Blitz Play, Catch Me If You Can, Challenging Tank), money totals (Treat Yo Self, $100,000 spent) and the cryptic hidden ones (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) — several of those had no confirmed method at launch, so each card says so honestly instead of guessing. Then Actions Speak Louder (500 Criminal Profile actions) should be close, and the platinum pops.
